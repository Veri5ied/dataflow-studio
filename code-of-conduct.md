# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Give actionable technical feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insults, or disruptive conduct.
- Sharing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation for conduct violations.

## Reporting

To report violations, open a private issue or contact maintainers through designated project channels.
